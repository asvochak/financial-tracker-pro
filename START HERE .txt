A simple system designed to help you take control of your money and get out of debt.
--------------------------------------------------

Take control of your money.
Track every bill. Know exactly where your money goes.

--------------------------------------------------

HOW TO USE:

1. Open "index.html" in your browser
2. Click "Start Tracking My Money"
3. Enter all of your accounts on the setup page
4. Click "View Dashboard"
5. Use the tracker each month:
   - Enter what is due
   - Enter what you paid
   - Track your progress and your remaining money

--------------------------------------------------

HOW IT WORKS:

• Setup Page → Define all of your accounts and categories  
• Dashboard → Track real monthly payments and progress  

This is not a spreadsheet.

This is a system designed to help you:
• Stop guessing  
• Stay accountable  
• Take control of your finances  

--------------------------------------------------

IMPORTANT NOTES:

• Do NOT rename or move the files  
• Your data is stored locally in your browser (nothing is uploaded)  
• Works best in Chrome or Edge  
• Your data stays completely private on your device
