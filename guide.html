AIBD Financial Tracker Pro – Guide

This system is designed to help you take control of your money and stay consistent month after month.

Step 1 – Setup

Go to the setup page and enter all of your accounts.

Step 2 – Track Monthly

Each month:

  • Select the current month
  • Enter what each bill is due
  • Enter what you actually paid

Step 3 – Review Progress

Use the Progress page to see your score and identify what needs improvement.

Important Notes

  • Your data is saved automatically on your device
  • Use Export Data to create a backup
  • You can Import Data anytime to restore
